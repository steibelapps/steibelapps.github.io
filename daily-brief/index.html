Daily Brief

Your day on one screen: weather, calendar, reminders, news, tonight's sky, and the wildlife that lives near you.

Get help

Write to steibelapps+dailybrief@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone you have, your iOS version, and what you expected to happen. You can also send feedback from Settings inside the app.

Common questions

The weather or wildlife is for the wrong place

Daily Brief uses your location to find local weather, tonight's sky and the animals near you. Open the iPhone's Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services, then Daily Brief, and choose While Using the App.

A widget looks out of date

iOS decides how often widgets refresh. Opening the app refreshes them right away. If one stays blank, remove it from the Home Screen and add it again.

Calendar or reminders are missing

Open the iPhone's Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Calendars (or Reminders), and give Daily Brief full access.

Managing your subscription

You get full access for your first 14 days. No payment details, no automatic charge. A subscription, once bought, renews until you cancel. Subscriptions are bought and billed through Apple: open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, to change or cancel one.

Playing trivia with someone on Android

A trivia round started in Messages is sent as a link. Anyone without the app, on any phone or computer, can tap the link and play the same questions in a browser.

Where the information comes from

Weather comes from Apple Weather and the US National Weather Service. Wildlife ranges are built from public US Geological Survey, US Fish and Wildlife Service and NatureServe data, and local bird and frog lists from the Global Biodiversity Information Facility. Sources are credited inside the app.
