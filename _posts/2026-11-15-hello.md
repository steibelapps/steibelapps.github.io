Hi, I'm Joe. I'm the creator and designer of Daily Brief and Periodic. I'm a full-time dad to my two young children and our two pups, and a loving husband to my beautiful wife. I also work a regular 9-to-5 job, five days a week.

I am by no means a professional programmer. What I do have is a curiosity that won't quit. I build these apps with AI, mostly Claude, in the quiet time before everyone wakes up.

This blog is the story of how that actually works.

I'm going to start from scratch. Daily Brief began as a simple idea on a dog walk and a first rough version in August 2026. Since then it has gone through more than a thousand changes. I have the whole history, so I'll walk through it step by step: what I asked for, what came back, what worked, and what didn't.

You'll get the "ha ha" moments, like the app proudly naming my yellow mums and then drawing them pink. You'll also get the "oh no, what did I just do" moments, like the night my Mac's hard drive filled up while I slept.

Along the way I'll share what I've learned about working with AI: what it's great at, where it still needs a person paying attention, and how a non-programmer with 15 spare minutes a morning can ship real apps.

Some posts will wander into real life too, like the yard, the house, the kids and the dogs, because that's where most of the ideas come from.

If you're curious about building something yourself, or you just like a good behind-the-scenes story, stick around. New posts go up on Sundays.
